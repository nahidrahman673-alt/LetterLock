# LetterLock

A SwiftUI party word game for iPhone. Each round, players get one letter and fill 10 categories with
words starting with it. Rare answers score most. Played in a room of 2 to 8 friends, joined with a
4-character room code.

## Rules (summary; the Rules sheet is the source of truth)

- 2 to 8 players. Host sets 5 or 3 rounds (default 5) and a 90 or 60 second timer (default 90).
- Players take turns choosing the letter: Shuffle picks A to Z at random; watching an ad reshuffles.
- 10 fixed categories, one per page: Boy's Name, Girl's Name, Country, City, Animal, Food/Drink,
  Movies/Series, Book/Movie Character, Celebrity, Brand.
- Lock In ends your answering. When 3 players lock in, the clock drops to 5 seconds.
- Scoring per category: unique 10, two the same 5 each, three or more 0, blank 0, vetoed 0.
  Matching ignores capitals and spaces; spelling is never checked.
- Reveal: one category at a time, one player at a time; points show once all are revealed.
  Veto starts a vote of everyone but the author; more rejects than accepts scores it 0, a tie keeps it.
- Highest total wins. Equal top totals are a draw with shared first place, no tie-break.
- Drop-outs keep their seat and score; missed answers are blank. If the host leaves, the next
  player becomes host. Whatever is typed at 0:00 counts.
- Only the host starts the game and rounds, moves the reveal on, and starts Play Again.

## Design

LetterLock theme only (never the old "Great Minds" style). Colours live in `LetterLock/Views/Theme.swift`:
navy #121324 background, surface #231E45, line #3A3368, field edge #7A72B8, cream #FFF7E6,
lavender #B9B2D9, yellow #FFD23F (primary buttons), mint #3DDBB4, coral #FF6B5B (Lock In, Veto),
lilac #B79CFF. Rounded bold type for titles and letter tiles. Primary buttons are 58pt tall with
16pt corners. Every screen after Home shows the LetterLock title bar with Leave and mute.

## Code

- MVVM: `Models/GameModels.swift`, `ViewModels/GameViewModel.swift` (all game logic, @Observable),
  `Views/` (one file per screen; `RainShuffle.swift` is the rain letter shuffle).
- Multiplayer is simulated on one phone for now (test players and stand-ins); there is no server yet.
- Audio: `Audio/AudioManager.swift` plays music and effects. All sounds are original and generated by
  `Tools/generate_audio.py`. Regenerate with `python3 -I Tools/generate_audio.py LetterLock/Audio`.
- Time's up sound: the owner is choosing between Dark Gong and Echo Bell (picker in the lobby). Once
  chosen, keep one and remove the picker.
- Swift 6, iOS deployment target 27.2. Build the `LetterLock` scheme from `LetterLock.xcodeproj`.
- Debug launch arguments: `-ShuffleDemo`, `-PileDemo`, `-DrawDemo`.
- `Demo/` holds screen recordings and screenshots; it is deliberately not committed.
